# CR-2009-017: Prevent Duplicate Adjustments for Migrated Corporate Accounts

## Change Information

- Change ID: CR-2009-017
- Requested By: Billing Operations and Revenue Assurance
- Implementation Date: August 14, 2009
- Status: Implemented
- Priority: High
- Change Type: Production Defect Resolution
- Affected Application: Northstar Postpaid Billing

## Problem Description

Following the migration of corporate accounts from the legacy billing platform, Revenue Assurance identified duplicate financial adjustments on a small group of finalized invoices.

Some usage records arrived after invoice finalization and were initially placed in `REJECTED` status. The standard nightly rerating process later selected those records automatically.

For affected migrated accounts, rerating could reverse previously billed usage and generate a new charge after the original invoice had already been exported to the financial ledger. This created differences between billing balances and general-ledger balances.

## Approved Change

The nightly rerating program must exclude a usage record from automatic rerating when all of the following conditions are true:

1. account_profile.account_type = 'CORPORATE'
2. account_profile.migration_source = 'LEGACY_BILLER'
3. invoice.status = 'FINALIZED'

When these conditions are met, the record must be inserted into the `rerate_exception` table with reason code:

LEGACY_CORP_FINAL_INVOICE

The record must remain unchanged until Billing Operations completes a manual review.

## Components Affected

The change affects:

- nightly_rerate.sh
- rerate_usage.pc
- account_profile
- usage_event
- invoice
- rerate_exception

## Implementation Notes

The eligibility check will be implemented in `rerate_usage.pc` after the account and invoice information has been retrieved but before the normal rerating function is invoked.

The batch script does not require a functional change. It will continue to invoke the rerating program using the existing parameters.

A summary count of records routed to manual review should be written to the nightly batch log.

## Business Rationale

Finalized invoices that have already been exported to the financial ledger must not be changed automatically for migrated corporate accounts. Manual review is required to determine whether the late usage should be billed through an adjustment, carried into a subsequent billing period, or written off.

## Temporary-Control Status

This exception is intended to be a temporary migration-stabilization control.

The rule may be removed after:

- All migrated corporate accounts have completed the stabilization period.
- No additional late usage remains from the legacy platform.
- Billing Operations and Revenue Assurance approve removal.
- Finance confirms that automated rerating will not create ledger discrepancies.

The date and owner for reviewing these retirement conditions are **to be confirmed**.

## Validation

Testing must confirm that:

- Normal consumer accounts continue through automatic rerating.
- Non-migrated corporate accounts continue through automatic rerating.
- Migrated corporate accounts with open invoices continue through automatic rerating.
- Migrated corporate accounts with finalized invoices are sent to manual review.
- The original usage record is not financially adjusted.
- The correct exception reason is stored.

## Known Limitation

This change does not determine whether the exception remains necessary after migration stabilization. A separate business review is required before the rule can be removed.
